## 2. Unanimous 1x design gaps - conceded, folded into SPEC in this commit

**S1 - `LISTEN/NOTIFY` recovery protocol.** All four voices, independently derived, named the same 1x failure: notifications are transient, and a restart, disconnect, or listen/commit race strands a queued row (which holds no lease to expire) silently and forever. Conceded without reservation - the packet treated NOTIFY as a queue in effect even though the exclusions row calls it a wake-up. SPEC now states: NOTIFY wakes work, rows determine work; claim-scan on startup, reconnect, and a low-frequency timer; control/kill rows ride the same sweep (bounding kill latency without notification delivery); idempotency keys and replay rules on every external side effect; state-derived recovery; crash-in-the-NOTIFY-window joins the acceptance fixtures.

**S2 - Off-host backup and clean-host restore acceptance.** All four voices: `pg_dump` + manifest on the same disk shares the host's fate; no destination, RPO/RTO, or drill was declared. Conceded. SPEC now requires encrypted off-host backups, a declared RPO/RTO, a clean-host restore drill (through to opening a historical run's evidence) before the first live request and on a standing cadence, and a statement of how expired artifacts age out of backup generations.
